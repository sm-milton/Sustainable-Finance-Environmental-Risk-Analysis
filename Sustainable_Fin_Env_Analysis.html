1 Introduction

1.1 Business context

I am an entry-level analyst at an investment firm that finances sustainable infrastructure and applies responsible-investment principles. My line manager asked for four pieces of work:

  1. Most common climate hazards affecting communities globally
  2. Number of planned projects and total funding sought, by region
  3. Ranked list of collaboration / investment opportunities
  4. Best model to predict environmental controversy involvement, with evaluation
